# Edit a tile and build a sentence

## Log in

Enter "alex@example.com" as the email and "speakup2024" as the password, then click "Log In".
Verify the "Your Projects" page lists "Daily Needs", then click it.
Verify the board opens with the title "Home" and a "New tile".

## Rename the tile

Click the pencil icon in the bottom bar to edit the board, then click "New tile".
Clear the "Tile Text" field, type "Water" and click "Save Changes".
Click the pencil icon again to stop editing and verify the tile now reads "Water".

## Build a sentence

Click the "Water" tile and verify "Water" appears in the sentence bar at the top.
Click the red trash button and verify the sentence bar is empty.
